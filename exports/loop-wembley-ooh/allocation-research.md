# Loop x Pleasing — Wembley OOH Creative Allocation

**Campaign**: Harry Styles Wembley (12 Jun – 12 Jul 2026)
**Booking**: 39 London Underground 48-sheet platforms, booked via MGOMD / Global / Talon
**Creatives**:
- **A** — "Screaming Encouraged" (Wembley-specific, concert energy)
- **B** — "Hear for a good time and a long time" (general Loop x Pleasing brand)

**Final allocation**: 20 A / 19 B (51.3% / 48.7%)

---

## 1. Brief recap

From the meeting transcript and pre-read:

- Overall goal: ~50/50 split between A and B.
- A is "more concert/Wembley Stadium specific" — weight A more heavily on routes to Wembley.
- Pepper A around — don't concentrate it in one corridor.
- Nothing booked at Wembley itself (audience must still arrive at the stadium, not see it as a destination here).
- At every multi-platform station, the audience must see both creatives — i.e. mix A and B at any station with multiple booked frames.

## 2. Wembley access primer (verified against TfL data)

The 90,000-capacity stadium is served by three tube stations, all confirmed via wembleystadium.com / wembleypark.com / tfl.gov.uk:

- **Wembley Park** (Jubilee + Metropolitan, Zone 4) — ~5 min walk via Olympic Way. Primary route. Two stops from Baker Street on the Met (≈12 min).
- **Wembley Central** (Bakerloo + London Overground, Zone 4) — ~13 min walk. Secondary route.
- **Wembley Stadium** (Chiltern Railways from Marylebone, ~9 min) — recommended *post*-show route by Wembley Park's own travel guidance (less Jubilee congestion). Not a tube line.

Implication: **Jubilee, Metropolitan, and Bakerloo are the three "Wembley-direct" lines** — but only when the booked platform's *direction* actually points toward the stadium.

## 3. Directional convention sanity-check (TfL-verified)

- **Jubilee line** uses *Northbound/Southbound* from Stanmore down to Green Park, then *Eastbound/Westbound* from Green Park out to Stratford. So:
  - At Kilburn, Baker St, Bond St, Green Park (north of the pivot): `NB = toward Stanmore = TOWARD Wembley Park`. `SB = toward Stratford = AWAY`.
  - At Southwark, London Bridge, Waterloo (east of the pivot): `WB = toward Stanmore via Green Park = TOWARD Wembley Park`. `EB = toward Stratford = AWAY`.
- **Metropolitan line** uses Eastbound = toward Aldgate, Westbound = toward outer termini (Uxbridge/Watford/Amersham/Chesham).
  - At Eastcote (Uxbridge branch), `Pf 2 EB = toward Aldgate via Wembley Park (6 stops) = TOWARD Wembley Park` (confirmed live on tfl.gov.uk Eastcote arrivals page).
- **Bakerloo line** runs Elephant & Castle ↔ Harrow & Wealdstone via Wembley Central. `NB = TOWARD Wembley Central`. `SB = AWAY`.

This means several frames the prior draft assigned A are in fact pointed *away* from Wembley (Southwark Jubilee EB, Baker St Jubilee SB, Waterloo Bakerloo SB, all four KX platforms). The new allocation corrects for that.

## 4. Classification rubric

Each booked platform is classified into one tier based on the *direction the audience standing on that specific platform is travelling*:

- **Tier 1 — Direct Wembley-bound platform**: Jubilee NB/WB toward Stanmore, Metropolitan EB toward Aldgate via Wembley Park, Bakerloo NB toward Harrow via Wembley Central. **Strong A**.
- **Tier 2 — One-change Wembley-feeder**: Platform direction takes the audience into a central interchange (Baker St, Bond St, Green Park, Oxford Circus, Waterloo, London Bridge, KX) where a single change reaches a Wembley-direct line. **A unless the same hub is already over-indexed**.
- **Tier 3 — Major arrival hub with no direct Wembley-bound platform booked**: National Rail termini (KX/St P, Waterloo, Liverpool St) and central tourist quarters where Wembley-fan footfall is high even on a wrong-direction platform. **A for at least one platform; B for diversity at duplicates**.
- **Tier 4 — Suburban / wrong-direction / general commuter**: Platform direction is away from central or toward a terminus with no Wembley relevance. **B**.

Constraints applied on top:

- Hit 20 A / 19 B (closest integer split to 50/50).
- All 8 direct-Wembley platforms = A (no exceptions).
- At every multi-platform station, at least one A and at least one B so audiences see both creatives.
