## Logic summary (for the agency body copy)

- All eight platforms physically pointed at Wembley Park / Wembley Central run **Creative A** (Jubilee NB, Metropolitan EB toward Aldgate, Bakerloo NB toward Harrow & Wealdstone).
- A second tier of nine platforms whose direction feeds the audience into a Wembley-line interchange within one change also run **A**.
- Three further A placements cover central arrival hubs (Liverpool Street, Kings Cross Piccadilly, Hyde Park Corner) where concert-goer footfall is high even though the platform itself isn't Wembley-direct.
- Every multi-platform station carries at least one **A** *and* at least one **B**, so audiences pass both creatives at the major interchanges (Bond St, Green Park, Baker St, Waterloo, London Bridge, Kings Cross).
- The remaining 19 platforms run **Creative B** — south-London suburban runs, wrong-direction frames at Wembley-line hubs, and the duplicate platforms held back at multi-line interchanges so the audience sees both creatives.
