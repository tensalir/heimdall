# Testimonials: how to use them correctly

## Do
- Shorten testimonials if space is limited **without changing meaning**.
- Always attribute: "These are life-changing!" -- Jessica G.
- If needed, mention product after the name: "Joe from Texas on Loop Quiet 2."

## Don't
- Never invent consumer quotes (UGC video editing excluded).
- Don't use generic signatures ("a happy Loop customer").
- Don't use fake profile pictures.
- Don't shorten in a way that alters the original meaning.

## In paid social context
- Testimonials work well as headlines or description lines: `"Literally changed my life..." -- verified buyer`
- Social proof numbers (reviews count, customer count) are separate from testimonials and don't need attribution.
