# Sensitivities, inclusivity, and off-limits topics

## Community sensitivities
- Don't generalize about ADHD/autism/neurodivergence/mental health or children.
- Avoid judgmental assumptions (e.g., implying parents without earplugs are impatient).
- Avoid "perfect for X group" absolutes; use specific, conditional language instead.
- When writing ADHD/noise-sensitivity ads, use empathetic framing: "ADHD got you feeling noise sensitive?" is OK; "ADHD people can't handle noise" is not.

## Humor
- Aim for wit/charm/positivity.
- Avoid cynicism, black humor, derogatory humor.

## Political topics
- You can participate authentically in LGBTQ+/Pride conversations, but do not commercially exploit them.
- Avoid off-limit topics including sustainability/climate change, current conflicts, activism, US elections, broad "equality" positioning, etc.

## Diversity and representation
- Seek maximum diversity across the volume of ads: genders, races, ages, body types, sexual orientations.
- Avoid thoughtless "inclusive" claims paired with imagery that can read as tokenizing/offensive.

## Tone guardrails
- We're a positive brand: don't lean into doom-and-gloom or make light of real-world crises.
