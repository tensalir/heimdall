# Loop copy voice (paid social context)

## Core voice markers
Loop paid social copy should be:

- **Bold, straightforward**: lead with the point; don't "warm up."
- **Human and conversational**: speak like a person; use contractions for flow.
- **Fun with a mature edge**: "fun and engaging, but more mature," typically written for educated 25-35.
- **Simple but not boring**: avoid jargon, tricky language, and hard-to-parse metaphors.
- **Positive**: you can name the problem, but product-related copy should land uplifting (avoid doom-and-gloom).
- **Witty/charming**: use wit, wordplay, light irony; never cynicism/derogation.
- **Premium**: experimentation is welcome, but it must still feel considered and "Loop."

## Patterns observed in high-performing ads

### 1) A sharp, big hook
- Question hooks: "Tired of counting sheep?" "Love the vibes, hate the noise?"
- Calling out intent: "FLIRTING WITH THE IDEA?" "Looking for earplugs you can commit to?"
- Playful identity hooks: "I'M ALL EARS" "Hello, bliss bubble"
- Micro-drama: "Snoring partner? Problem solved." "Thin walls got you like..."
- Social proof leads: "The reviews are in!" "Over 2 MILLION+ customers use Loop..."

### 2) A quick benefit line (use-case specific)
- "Focus on your date without the distractions."
- "Filters loud noises... from first lift to apres."
- "Noise-reducing earplugs for better sleep."
- "Turn down noisy distractions so you can live life at your volume."

### 3) One trust cue
- Rating/reviews, returns, or other proof points (when available).
- "Try today with 100-day, hassle-free returns."
- "57,000+ 5-star ratings"
- "Join over 13 million happy customers."

## "Loop-y" wordbank (safe defaults)
- focus, calm, clear, comfortable, distraction-free, filter, take the edge off, tune in, dial down noise, stay present, sweet dreams, peace and quiet, hear the difference, live life at your volume, level up, vibe out
- avoid: "life-changing" unless it's a *real* attributed testimonial (see `compliance/testimonials.md`).

## Tone dials (choose explicitly per brief)
- **Flirty / playful**: dating metaphors, teasing hooks, but keep it tasteful. Good for: generic, fashion, brand-level.
- **Practical / confident**: quick benefit-proof; minimal attitude. Good for: comparison, quiz funnel, DPA.
- **Calm / soothing** (Dream/Sleep): sensory, gentle cadence, reassurance.
- **Sport / adventure** (Engage/Experience): energetic, crisp, outdoorsy language.
- **Parental / empathetic** (Engage Kids/Parenting): warm, understanding, never judgmental.
- **Hype / urgency** (BFCM/seasonal): excitement-driven, deal-focused, but still Loop-premium.

## What to avoid (copy-level)
- Teen-slang drift (unless targeting teens).
- Cliches and overpoetic lines.
- Colloquialisms/idioms that non-native speakers may not get.
- Screamer copy / excessive shouting.
- Generic, interchangeable ad copy that could be any earplug brand.
