# Comms hierarchy (Loop paid social)

Use this sequence as your default structure for every paid social ad:

1) **Capture attention**
Start with a clear, bold, engaging hook that demonstrates immediate value. In paid social, this is the first line of your caption. it must stop the scroll.

2) **Drive relevance and desire**
Expand on the lifestyle benefit: show not just what Loop does, but how it makes life better/easier/more enjoyable. Back benefits with insights when possible. In paid social, this is typically the body of your caption. often as an emoji-bulleted list.

3) **Build trust and credibility**
Use proof points: reviews/ratings, 100-day free returns, R&D/patents, expert or user testimonials. In paid social, this often lives in the description line or final caption line.

## Performance heuristics
- Hook quality is decisive; be audience-centered (their problem/search language).
- Keep copy relatable: everyday situations; what it feels like with/without the product.
- Avoid jargon; write in the same language customers use.
- Default to at least 4 variations for testing.
