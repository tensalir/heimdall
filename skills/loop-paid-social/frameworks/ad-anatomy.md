# Ad anatomy: patterns from 295 real Loop paid social ads

This document distills structural and stylistic patterns from Loop's entire paid social copy library. Use it as your baseline when writing new ads. These patterns represent what Loop consistently does, not rigid rules.

## Caption (primary text)

### Length
- Average: ~250 characters
- Range: 83-576 characters
- Short captions (under 150 chars) are punchy one-liners with a CTA
- Long captions (400+ chars) are emoji-bulleted feature/benefit lists

### Structure patterns

**Pattern A: Question hook + emoji list + CTA** (most common, ~40% of ads)
```
[Question or call-out hook] [emoji]
[emoji] Benefit 1
[emoji] Benefit 2
[emoji] Benefit 3
[emoji] Benefit 4
[CTA line with full stop.]
```
Example: "Love the vibes, hate the noise? 😫 Loop Experience™ earplugs are made for music lovers.\n🎶 Ideal for live music\n💎 Crystal-clear sound quality\n🧼 Washable and reusable\nGot a lot of gigs on the agenda? Pick up a pair today."

**Pattern B: Narrative hook + short body + proof + CTA** (~30% of ads)
```
[Bold opening statement or question] [emoji]
[1-2 sentences expanding the scenario/benefit]
[Proof point or returns mention]
[CTA with full stop.]
```
Example: "Bye, restless nights! 👋 Loop earplugs are here to level up your bedtime routine. Just pop them in and enjoy some sweet, sweet peace and quiet.\nDon't waste another night tossing and turning. Try them with 100-day, hassle-free returns. Sweet dreams! 😴"

**Pattern C: Social proof lead + features + CTA** (~15% of ads)
```
[Social proof statement] [emoji]
[Brief product intro]
[emoji] Feature 1
[emoji] Feature 2
[CTA.]
```
Example: "😌 Over 2 MILLION+ customers use Loop earplugs to reduce triggering noise and feel calmer throughout the day.\nTry for yourself with 100-day, hassle-free returns 👋\n🌍 2 MILLION+ customers\n🚀 #1 earplugs on the internet\n⭐️ 34,000+ 5-star reviews"

**Pattern D: Minimalist hook + single CTA** (~15% of ads)
```
[Short, punchy statement.] [emoji] [One-line benefit or proof.] [CTA.]
```
Example: "It's noisy out there 🙉 Cut through the chatter and focus on what matters with the internet's favorite earplugs."

### Hook analysis
- 51% of ads use a question hook (highest engagement pattern)
- Question hooks dominate in: Sleep, NoiseSensitive, Focus, Travel
- Call-out hooks dominate in: Experience, MusicNightlife
- Social proof hooks dominate in: Generic, Comparison
- Playful/identity hooks: Fashion, Kinetic, Sublime collections

### Emoji usage
- 99% of captions use at least one emoji
- Emojis-as-bullets: most common pattern (43% of all ads). **Keep bullet lists to 3-4 items max, each bullet ~5-8 words.** Short and snappy, not exhaustive feature dumps.
- Common bullet emojis: ⭐️ ✅ 🙌 ☁️ 💪 🎶 🔊 👍
- Hook emojis: 😵‍💫 😫 🙉 😴 😱 🤯 💖
- CTA emojis: 👇 👋 💥 🤩 🎉

## Headline

### Length
- Average: 31 characters
- Range: ~15-50 characters

### Key rule: No asset echo
The headline must NOT repeat or paraphrase the text on the visual asset. If the asset says "Sleep like never before," the headline should go broader on theme or hit a different angle (proof point, CTA, product benefit). The headline should work across all variants in the batch, not mirror any single asset.

**No full stops in headlines.** If the headline ends with an emoji, the emoji acts as the full stop.

### Patterns
- **Benefit statement + emoji**: "Less noise, more fun 🎵" / "Work smarter with Loop 📈"
- **Social proof**: "57,000+ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ratings" / "13M+ happy customers"
- **Playful/emotional**: "Earplugs your ears will 💗" / "Comfy like cloud nine ☁️"
- **Question/curiosity**: "Ready to hear the difference? 🙉"
- **Testimonial snippet**: "Literally changed my life..."

### Headline-description pairing
Headlines and descriptions work as a unit. Common pairings:
- Emotional headline + proof description: "Earplugs that elevate your sleep game 🌃" + "2-year warranty"
- Proof headline + benefit description: "57,000+ 5-star ratings" + "Engineered for enjoyment"
- CTA headline + trust description: "Shop on sale" + "100-day, hassle-free returns"

## Description (link description)

### Length
- Target: 15-20 characters (this field is tiny in Meta's UI)
- Use abbreviations: "13M+" not "13 million+", "57k+" not "57,000+"
- **No full stops in descriptions**

### Current stats for descriptions
Use these updated proof points in new copy (the historical examples below show older numbers from the ad archive):
- "22M+ pairs sold"
- "57k+ 5-star ratings"
- "13M+ customers"
- "2-year warranty"
- "100-day free returns"
- Switch specific: "1.73M units sold"

### Top descriptions by frequency (historical)
1. "100-day, hassle-free returns" (22 uses)
2. "10 million+ happy fans" (21 uses)
3. "55,000+ 5-star reviews" (14 uses)
4. "2-year warranty" (7 uses)
5. "Risk-free returns" (6 uses)
6. "Find your perfect pair" (6 uses)

The description line is almost always a proof point or trust signal, not a benefit claim.

## Product-specific patterns

### Dream / Sleep ads
- Tone: calm, soothing, gentle
- Common hooks: "Tired of counting sheep?" "Snoring partner?" "Need a power-up?"
- Emojis: 😴 💤 ☁️ 🌙 🛌
- Focus: comfort, noise reduction power, side-sleeper approved

### Experience / Music ads
- Tone: energetic, cool, adventurous
- Common hooks: "Love the vibes, hate the noise?" "Attention nightlife lovers:"
- Emojis: ⚡️ 🎶 🎸 💎 🤘
- Focus: sound quality, certified protection, festival-ready

### Engage / Social ads
- Tone: warm, relatable, social
- Common hooks: "Looking for earplugs you can commit to?" "Hate loud restaurants?"
- Emojis: 💖 🍻 🗣 🎤
- Focus: conversation clarity, social situations, discreet design

### Quiet / Focus ads
- Tone: calm but confident, productivity-oriented
- Common hooks: "What do you secretly want?" "Did you know earplugs can boost productivity?"
- Emojis: 🤫 🧑‍💻 📈 🦋
- Focus: noise reduction, deep work, travel

### Switch ads
- Tone: versatile, tech-savvy
- Common hooks: "Noise sensitive and looking for a multiple-mode earplug?"
- Emojis: 🤹 🔊 👂
- Focus: 3 modes, versatility, customizable

### Kids ads
- Tone: warm, playful, parent-to-parent
- Common hooks: "Want to give your kid a boost?" "The reviews are in!"
- Emojis: 🚀 🦖 🤖 🦄 🐙
- Focus: learning/playing/growing, comfort for little ears, parent-approved

## Campaign-specific patterns

### BFCM / Sale ads
- Lead with urgency: "Yep, the sale of the year is ready and waiting for you 👀"
- Include discount info prominently
- Still maintain Loop voice (not generic sale-speak)

### Collab ads (Tomorrowland, Coachella, etc.)
- Lead with the partner brand/event
- Limited-edition urgency: "Get your limited-edition pair before they run out"
- Contextual proof: festival-specific benefits

### Collection launches (Kinetic, Sublime)
- Lead with the aesthetic/vibe of the collection
- Color-focused: name the specific colorways
- Personality-driven: "Don't look back in anger 🙌" / "Rebel against noise, stress, and overwhelm ✨"
